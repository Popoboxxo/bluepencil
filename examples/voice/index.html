Voice dictation fixture

Pick the text annotation mode in the layer bar, then click this paragraph. The composer opens with a microphone button that drives the host-injected recogniser.

What this page proves

  • Final transcripts are appended to the composer, interim results only reach the live region.
  • Nothing is saved automatically — a human still presses Save.
  • With ?voice=0 there is no microphone, no status region and no recogniser.
